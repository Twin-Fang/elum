FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Email us below.

Who is ELUM for?
We made it with people who understand pictures and order more easily than text in mind — people with developmental disabilities or on the autism spectrum, people with ADHD or learning disabilities who like to see their steps at a glance — and the guardians and teachers who look after the day with them. We don't divide users by age.
Does it work on both iPhone and Android?
Yes. Get it on the App Store or Google Play. The guardian's phone and Elumi's phone can be different kinds and still link.
Where do I get the link code?
Make a link code on the guardian screen, then enter it on Elumi's phone to connect the two. Forgot it? Make a new one on the guardian screen — the old code stops working from then on.
How do I go back to the guardian screen?
Switching from Elumi's screen needs the passcode you set at the start, so settings can't be changed by accident.
Cards aren't being made
Check your internet connection first. If it still fails, email us the error code shown on screen (for example E-1001) so we can find where it stopped.
The cards read a little odd
Cards are made by AI and only reach Elumi after the guardian checks them. Edit the wording or make them again on the guardian screen.
Where does what I write go?
The situation and answers you write are sent as written to an AI provider to make the cards, so please don't include names, phone numbers, addresses or anything that identifies a person. The text is also kept on the ELUM server so you can review and edit it, and it's deleted when you delete your account. Details are in the privacy policy (Korean).
How do I delete my account?
Tap Delete account in the guardian screen's settings. Routines, Elumi's information and stars are deleted right away and can't be restored. To stop free usage limits from being claimed again, minimal data such as the login identifier and AI usage count is kept for one year and then deleted. To have it deleted sooner, email chan4760@gmail.com. See the account deletion guide (Korean).
Elumi got a new phone
Install the app on the new phone, make a new link code on the guardian screen and enter it.
