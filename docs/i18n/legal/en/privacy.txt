0. Who Enters Information, and Who Consents

In ELUM (이룸), a guardian creates the account and enters the Elumi's information on the Elumi's behalf. Multiple guardians (including family members and social workers)
may be linked to one Elumi, and all linked guardians enter the Elumi's
information and view routines together. The Elumi does not create an account and does not
sign in directly.

To enter information on the Elumi's behalf, each guardian must fall under one of the following.
- If the Elumi is under 14 years of age (만 14세) — the guardian is the legal representative (법정대리인) (a parent or legal guardian (후견인)).
  This consent substitutes for the consent of the legal representative required by the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea (개인정보 보호법).
- If the Elumi is an adult and legal guardianship (후견) has commenced — the guardian is a court-appointed adult legal guardian (성년후견인) or
  limited legal guardian (한정후견인).
- If the Elumi is an adult and legal guardianship has not commenced — the guardian enters information on the Elumi's behalf with the consent of
  the Elumi. In principle, an adult may consent on their own, and in this case the guardian
  is not a legal representative.

Any linked guardian can edit the Elumi's information in the app, and the most recently changed
value is kept. A routine can be edited or deleted only by the guardian who created it.

1. Items Collected

[Account]
- Sign-in provider (Kakao, Naver, Google, Apple) and the member identifier issued by the provider
- Email address
- Guardian nickname (created automatically at sign-up; the guardian can change it)
- Date and time of most recent sign-in, number of sign-ins

[Staying Signed In]
- Transformed value of the token used to keep you signed in, device identifier

[Elumi Information] — entered directly by the guardian
- The name used to call the Elumi (nickname)
- Selected character
- Support goals

[Routines]
- The situation description and answers to follow-up questions entered by the guardian
- Titles and steps of the created cards, scheduled date and time, rewards, completion date and time

[Usage Records]
- AI call type, model used, response time, throughput, estimated cost

[Send Feedback] — only when the guardian sends feedback from Settings
- The feedback text written by the guardian
- App version, operating system
- App status log (you can choose whether to send it; it is on by default) — a record of recent app
  activity, containing the content of requests and responses exchanged with the server, screen navigation, and errors.
  The response content may include information within the app, such as the name used to call the Elumi and routine
  content. Sign-in tokens and passcodes are masked before sending.
- Phone information (sent together under the same checkbox as the app status log; on by default)
  — where the app was installed from (whether installed from a store), the app signing key value, a random number generated by the app
  at installation, phone manufacturer, model and operating system version, time zone and region, network
  connection type, app build type and running time, whether low power mode is on, remaining storage space,
  whether KakaoTalk is installed and its version, Kakao sign-in setting values, and the last social sign-in error.
  Phone unique numbers (IMEI, serial number), advertising identifiers, location, contacts, and the list of other installed apps
  are not collected for this purpose.

[Community] — only when the guardian shares a routine or reacts in Community
- The title, card text, and card images of the shared routine (for photos taken by the guardian, the guardian can choose whether to
  blur them), text written by the guardian, and category
- Comments, "Helpful" reactions, and records of taking routines
- Reports and records of hiding guardians

[Advertising — collected directly by Google on the Guardian mode screen]
- Advertising identifier (iOS advertising identifier, Android advertising ID)
- IP address and approximate location based on it
- Device and app information (operating system, device model, app version)
- Ad usage information such as ad impressions and clicks
- App crash and performance diagnostic information

2. Items Not Collected
We do not collect diagnoses, disability type or grade, resident registration numbers, contact information, addresses, precise location information (GPS),
or photo library or contacts access information. The transfer to Google of approximate location based on the IP address
in the course of displaying advertisements is as described in the advertising item above.
Personalization is based only on the "support goals" selected by the guardian.

3. Purposes of Use
- Member identification and keeping you signed in
- Creating and personalizing routine cards
- Recording task completion and providing rewards
- Operating the service and responding to failures
- Reviewing feedback sent by guardians and identifying the causes of problems
- Providing features in Community for guardians to share routines and exchange reactions, and reviewing reports
- Ad serving, ad performance measurement, fraud prevention (Google)

[Visibility of Content Shared in Community]
Routines, posts, and comments shared in Community are visible to other signed-in guardians, and other guardians
can take them as their own routines. The guardian nickname is shown as the author; email and
sign-in information are not shown. The name used to call the Elumi is replaced with "Elumi" when
posted. Please make sure faces, name tags, and addresses are not visible in photos.

4. Retention Period
- Upon withdrawal from membership, the email address, date and time of most recent sign-in and number of sign-ins, sign-in tokens, and the
  routines created by that guardian are destroyed without delay.
- The Elumi's information and the Elumi's phone link information remain if another guardian is also linked,
  and are destroyed together when no linked guardian remains. Stars collected by the Elumi and routines created by other
  guardians remain as they are.
- Feedback text, app status logs, and phone information are destroyed without delay upon withdrawal from membership. Even before then,
  they may be deleted once the review of the problem is complete.
- Routines, posts, and comments shared in Community, and records of reactions, reports, and hiding, are destroyed without delay when the guardian takes them down or upon withdrawal
  from membership. Routines already taken by another guardian remain as that guardian's
  routines.
- Where laws and regulations prescribe a separate retention period, the information is retained for that period.

[Information Retained for 1 Year After Withdrawal]
The following information is retained for 1 year from the date of withdrawal and then destroyed.
- Account identifiers (social sign-in provider and its member identification number, service ID) and AI feature
  usage records (date, time and number of uses) — retained to prevent abuse in which the free usage limit is
  repeatedly obtained by withdrawing and re-registering.
- If registered with a service ID, the transformed value of the password — retained to verify identity and restore the previous account
  if the user signs in again with the same ID within this period.
- Records of consent to the terms (whether consented, date and time of consent, version of the document consented to) — retained to prove what was consented to
  and when.

During retention, this information is not used for any purpose other than those stated above. If you
re-register with the same account within this period, the previous account is restored in an empty state and the usage records
continue. The Elumi's information and routines are not restored, and consent to the terms is obtained again.
If you request immediate deletion of retained information, it will be destroyed without delay.

When destroyed at the end of the retention period, AI call records (call type, model, response time, throughput,
cost) are disconnected from the member so that it cannot be known whose records they are, and are kept only as service operation
statistics. These records do not contain routine content or the Elumi's information.

5. Rights of Guardians
Any linked guardian can edit the Elumi's information in the app, and a routine can be deleted by the guardian who
created it. A guardian can unlink (leave) the Elumi in the app.
Deletion can be requested by withdrawing from membership. Requests sent to chan4760@naver.com will also be processed, and immediate deletion of
information retained after withdrawal may also be requested at this address.

6. Right to Refuse Consent
You may refuse this consent. However, because an account and routine cards cannot be created,
you cannot use the service.

7. Security Measures
- Passwords are stored after being transformed into a form that cannot be decrypted.
- For tokens used to keep you signed in, the original is not stored; only the transformed value is kept, and it is replaced with
  a new value each time it is renewed. If an already used token is submitted again, it is treated as stolen and
  all sign-in sessions of that account are terminated.
- All communication between the server and the app is encrypted (HTTPS).

8. Entrustment of Processing
When routine cards are created, processing is entrusted to the companies below.
- Google LLC — generating card text, generating card images
- OpenAI, L.L.C. — generating card text, generating card images
- Features & Labels Inc. (fal) — generating card images

The information entrusted is the situation description and answers to follow-up questions written by the guardian, the name used to call the Elumi
(nickname), and the selected support goals. Because sentences written by the guardian are transferred as they are, without modification,
please do not include information that can identify a person, such as names, phone numbers,
or addresses.

The server is operated directly by ELUM and is not entrusted to any external party.

[Advertising Service]
Advertisements are displayed on the Guardian mode screen through Google LLC's advertising service (Google AdMob).
- Advertisements appear on the Guardian mode screens (Home, drafts list, credit information) in banner, native, and rewarded
  (ads that grant credits when watched to the end) formats, and do not appear on the Elumi mode screen or during the routine creation
  process.
- Information entered in the app, such as the Elumi's name, routines, and card content, is not used for advertising or transferred to
  Google.
- On iOS, you can refuse in the tracking permission prompt shown at first launch; if you refuse,
  non-personalized ads are displayed. You can change this in Settings > Privacy & Security >
  Tracking. If you refuse tracking on iOS, the advertising identifier is not used and only non-personalized
  ads are displayed.
- On Android, you can reset or delete the advertising ID in Settings > Google >
  Ads.
- How Google uses advertising information can be viewed at
  policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites .

9. Overseas Transfer
Because the companies above are located in the United States, personal information is transferred overseas.
- Recipients            Google LLC (policies.google.com/privacy)
                        OpenAI, L.L.C. (openai.com/policies/privacy-policy)
                        Features & Labels Inc. (fal.ai/legal/privacy-policy)
- Destination country   United States
- Time and method       Encrypted and transmitted via HTTPS each time a card is created
- Items transferred     The situation description and answers to follow-up questions written by the guardian, the name used to call
                        the Elumi (nickname), the selected support goals
- Purposes              Generating the text of routine cards, generating card images
- Retention period      In accordance with the recipient companies' policies; ELUM does not retain
                        the original request after receiving the response

You may refuse the transfer. However, because creating cards with AI is a core feature of ELUM,
you cannot use the service if you refuse.

[Overseas Transfer Related to Advertising]
- Recipient             Google LLC (policies.google.com/privacy)
- Destination country   United States
- Time and method       Transmitted via HTTPS each time an ad is loaded on the Guardian mode screen
- Items transferred     Advertising identifier, IP address (approximate location), device and app information, ad usage information,
                        crash and performance diagnostic information
- Purposes              Ad serving, ad performance measurement, fraud prevention
- Retention period      In accordance with Google's policies
You can refuse or reset the advertising identifier using the methods described above, and refusing does not affect
your use of the service.
