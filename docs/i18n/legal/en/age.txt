ELUM (이룸) accounts are created and used by guardians. We confirm that the person creating the account
is 14 years of age or older (만 14세).

This confirmation concerns the guardian who creates the account. There is no age limit for the individual using ELUM (the Elumi)
— even if the Elumi is under 14 years of age, they can use ELUM because the guardian enters information
on their behalf.

For the authority to enter the Elumi's information on their behalf, please see the "Collection and Use of Personal Information"
item.
