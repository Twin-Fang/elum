Article 2 (Description of the Service)
ELUM is an app that helps individuals with developmental disabilities carry out their routines. When a guardian enters a situation,
AI creates step-by-step cards, which appear on the individual's screen after the guardian has reviewed them.

- Results created by AI are shown to the individual only after the guardian approves them.
- Each card contains only one action.
- ELUM does not provide treatment or diagnosis. It does not replace consultation with a professional.
