## 자세한 설명 (두 스토어 공용, 4000)

```
ELUM is an action card app that helps with everyday life. It is a visual schedule that shows the day's routine as action cards, one step at a time.

When a guardian writes a situation such as "wash up and get dressed in the morning", ELUM splits it into easy steps and turns them into action cards. Cards appear on Elumi's screen only after the guardian has checked them.

■ Made with these people in mind
· People with developmental disabilities or on the autism spectrum who find pictures and order easier than text
· People with ADHD or learning disabilities who want to see the order of tasks at a glance
· Guardians who want to go through the day's routine step by step
· Teachers in parenting and special education who use action cards and visual schedules

■ One action per card
Each card holds just one action. It shows a picture and a short sentence, and reads it out loud. Check it off when it's done, and get the reward you set at the end.

■ Part of everyday life
Turn the things you do every day into a visual schedule: getting ready for school, morning routines, washing and brushing teeth, getting dressed, getting ready to go out, bedtime routines and tidying up.

■ Guardian mode and Elumi mode
· Guardian mode: make routines, edit action cards and change their order. See how much was done today
· Elumi mode: see today's tasks one action card at a time and check them off
· Going back from Elumi mode to Guardian mode needs a passcode
· Link two devices and the routines the guardian makes show up on Elumi's device
· Invite family or teachers to look after routines together

■ Daily routines in one go
Reuse past routines as they are, so routines you repeat every day are easy to prepare.

■ Please don't enter personal information
What you write when making cards is sent to AI. Please don't enter anything that can identify a person, such as names, phone numbers or addresses. What we collect and how we handle it is in the in-app settings and the privacy policy.

You can find the contact address under Contact us in the app's settings.
```
