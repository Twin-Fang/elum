■ ELUM Pro subscription
· ELUM Pro is an auto-renewing subscription, billed monthly or yearly. New subscribers get a 7-day free trial.
· Payment is charged to your Apple ID account when you confirm the purchase. The subscription renews automatically for the same period and price unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
· Manage or cancel your subscription in Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions.
