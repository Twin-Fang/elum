ELUM (이룸) turns a guardian's plain-language description of a daily routine into simple step-by-step cards for a person with a developmental disability. The app is Korean-only.

HOW TO SIGN IN
The app offers Sign in with Apple, Kakao and Naver. We are not able to hand over a shared Kakao account, so please tap "Apple로 계속하기" (Continue with Apple) on the first screen and use your own Apple ID. Nothing beyond what Sign in with Apple returns is requested, and the account can be removed at any time from 보호자 설정 (Guardian settings) > 탈퇴하기.

SUGGESTED REVIEW FLOW (about 3 minutes)
1. Tap "Apple로 계속하기" and sign in.
2. On "이 휴대폰은 누가 사용하나요?" choose 보호자 (Guardian).
3. Set a 4-digit 비밀암호 (PIN) and keep it. It is needed to return from the user screen.
4. On the home screen tap "새로운 일과 만들기" (Create a new routine).
5. Type any short routine in Korean, for example: 아침에 세수하고 옷 입기 (wash up and get dressed in the morning). Tap the suggestion chips if you prefer not to type Korean.
6. The server generates the step cards from the text. Review them and save.
7. Switch to 이룸이 화면 (the user screen) to see the cards one at a time. Returning to the guardian screen asks for the PIN from step 3.

WHAT THE APP DOES NOT DO
- No in-app purchase.
- Ads (Google AdMob banners) appear only on the guardian's own screens: the home screen and the saved-drafts list. There are no ads on the user screen (이룸이 화면), while creating a routine, or on the reward/completion screens. This is not a Kids Category app and its audience is guardians.
- Right after a fresh install, as soon as the app opens (before sign-in, on the first screen "이 휴대폰은 누가 사용하나요?"), the app shows the App Tracking Transparency prompt once. Declining it does not change any feature; the app then requests non-personalized ads. The prompt is not shown again once answered.
- No ad request contains the routine text, names or any information entered in the app.
- We do not collect diagnoses or disability types.
- The guardian's text is sent to an AI provider only to generate the cards. The app asks guardians not to enter personal information, and the raw text is not stored in any audit log.
- AI-generated cards are always confirmed by the guardian before the user screen shows them.
