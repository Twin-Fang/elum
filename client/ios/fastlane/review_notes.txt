ELUM (이룸) turns a guardian's plain-language description of a daily routine into simple step-by-step cards for a person with a developmental disability. Screens follow the device language (Korean, English, Japanese, Simplified Chinese or Spanish). Button names are in English with the Korean label in parentheses.

HOW TO SIGN IN
Tap "Log in with Apple" (Apple로 로그인) on the first screen and use your own Apple ID. Kakao and Naver sign-in are also offered, but we cannot hand over a shared account. Only what Sign in with Apple returns is requested. The account can be deleted in My info (내 정보) > gear icon (설정) > Delete account (회원탈퇴).

DEVICES
iPhone and iPad (all orientations, Split View). Elumi mode (이룸이 화면, the user screen) is mostly used on a tablet, so every screen adapts to the iPad window.

REVIEW FLOW (about 3 minutes)
1. Log in with Apple. 2. On "Who will use this device?" (이 기기는 누가 사용하나요?) choose Guardian (보호자). 3. Set a 4-digit passcode (비밀암호); it is needed to return from Elumi mode. 4. Home > "Make a new routine" (새로운 일과 만들기). 5. Type a short routine, e.g. 아침에 세수하고 옷 입기, or tap a suggestion chip. 6. Review the generated cards and save. 7. Switch to Elumi mode to see the cards one at a time; returning asks for the passcode.

IN-APP PURCHASE (auto-renewable subscription group "Elum Pro")
- Pro Monthly (elum_pro_monthly) and Pro Yearly (elum_pro_yearly), with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers.
- Purchase screen: Guardian > My info (내 정보) > My plan (내 요금제) > "Learn about Pro" (Pro 알아보기). It also opens when AI credits run out or a Pro-only feature is tapped.
- Subscribe: pick a plan, tap "Start 7 days free" (7일 무료로 시작하기), enter the guardian passcode, then confirm on the App Store sheet (Sandbox account).
- Same screen: Restore purchases (구매 복원), Terms of Use (이용약관), Privacy Policy (개인정보처리방침). Manage in Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions.
- Pro adds: more monthly AI credits, free picture redraws, a weekly report, repeat schedules, the guardian's own recorded voice (microphone permission is asked only when tapping Record, 녹음), print layouts, and no ads. The core flow above works without a subscription.

ADS AND PRIVACY
- Google AdMob banners appear only on the guardian's own screens (home, saved drafts), never on the user screen, while creating a routine, or on reward screens. Pro subscribers see no ads. Not a Kids Category app; the audience is guardians.
- On first launch, before sign-in, the App Tracking Transparency prompt is shown once. Declining changes no feature (non-personalized ads). No ad request contains routine text, names or entered information.
- We do not collect diagnoses or disability types. The guardian's text goes to an AI provider only to generate cards; the app asks guardians not to enter personal information, and raw text is not stored in audit logs. Guardians confirm AI cards before the user screen shows them.

COMMUNITY (이야기 tab in the guardian bottom bar)
Guardians share finished routines and import others'. Guardian-only content, never shown on the user screen. The first post or comment requires agreeing to the Community rules. Report: ⋯ > Report (신고하기). Block: ⋯ > Hide this guardian's posts (이 보호자 글 숨기기), restorable under My info > Hidden guardians. Our team reviews reports and removes rule-breaking content. Names are replaced with 이룸이 before posting.
