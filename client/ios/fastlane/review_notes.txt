ELUM (이룸) turns a guardian's plain-language description of a daily routine into simple step-by-step cards for a person with a developmental disability. The app's screens follow the device language: Korean or English (any other device language shows English). Button names below are given in English with the Korean label in parentheses.

HOW TO SIGN IN
The app offers Sign in with Apple, Kakao and Naver. We are not able to hand over a shared Kakao account, so please tap "Log in with Apple" (Apple로 로그인) on the first screen and use your own Apple ID. Nothing beyond what Sign in with Apple returns is requested, and the account can be removed at any time from My info (내 정보) > the gear icon (Settings, 설정) > Delete account (회원탈퇴).

DEVICES
The app runs on iPhone and iPad (all orientations, including Split View). The people who use Elumi mode (이룸이 화면, the user screen) mostly use a tablet, so every screen adapts to the iPad window size. The sign-in, guardian and user screens can all be reviewed on iPad in the same way as on iPhone.

SUGGESTED REVIEW FLOW (about 3 minutes)
1. Tap "Log in with Apple" (Apple로 로그인) and sign in.
2. On "Who will use this device?" (이 기기는 누가 사용하나요?) choose Guardian (보호자).
3. Set a 4-digit passcode (비밀암호) and keep it. It is needed to return from Elumi mode.
4. On the home screen tap "Make a new routine" (새로운 일과 만들기).
5. Type any short routine in Korean, for example: 아침에 세수하고 옷 입기 (wash up and get dressed in the morning). Tap the suggestion chips if you prefer not to type Korean.
6. The server generates the step cards from the text. Review them and save.
7. Switch to Elumi mode (이룸이 화면, the user screen) to see the cards one at a time. Returning to Guardian mode asks for the passcode from step 3.

WHAT THE APP DOES NOT DO
- No in-app purchase.
- Ads (Google AdMob banners) appear only on the guardian's own screens: the home screen and the saved-drafts list. There are no ads on the user screen (이룸이 화면), while creating a routine, or on the reward/completion screens. This is not a Kids Category app and its audience is guardians.
- Right after a fresh install, as soon as the app opens (before sign-in, on the first screen "Who will use this device?" (이 기기는 누가 사용하나요?)), the app shows the App Tracking Transparency prompt once. Declining it does not change any feature; the app then requests non-personalized ads. The prompt is not shown again once answered.
- No ad request contains the routine text, names or any information entered in the app.
- We do not collect diagnoses or disability types.
- The guardian's text is sent to an AI provider only to generate the cards. The app asks guardians not to enter personal information, and the raw text is not stored in any audit log.
- AI-generated cards are always confirmed by the guardian before the user screen shows them.

COMMUNITY (Community tab, 이야기, in the guardian bottom bar)
Guardians can share a finished routine with other guardians and import routines shared by others. Content is user-generated by guardians only; the user screen (이룸이 화면) never shows it.
- Before posting or commenting for the first time, a guardian must agree to the Community rules (no personal information, respect, no diagnoses).
- Reporting: tap ⋯ on a post or on a comment > Report (신고하기) and choose a reason.
- Blocking: ⋯ > Hide this guardian's posts (이 보호자 글 숨기기). Hidden guardians can be restored under My info > Hidden guardians (내 정보 > 숨긴 보호자).
- Our team reviews reports and removes content that breaks the rules.
- Names in shared routines are replaced with 이룸이 before posting.
