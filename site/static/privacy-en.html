Privacy Policy

This is an English translation for reference. If it differs from the Korean original, the Korean original prevails.

Effective November 12, 2026 · Last revised November 5, 2026

ELUM is an app that helps people with developmental disabilities (hereinafter “Elumi”) carry out their daily routines: a guardian creates cards, and the Elumi follows those cards through the day. ELUM does not collect diagnoses or disability types. Personalization is based only on the 'support goals' selected by the guardian.

2. Purposes of Processing Personal Information

  • Identifying members and keeping them signed in — account information, social sign-in identifier, token used to keep you signed in
  • Creating and personalizing routine cards — the Elumi's name, character and support goals, situation description entered by the guardian
  • Recording completion and providing rewards — whether routines were completed, number of stars
  • Operating the service and responding to failures — usage records, error records
  • Reviewing feedback sent by guardians and identifying the cause of problems — feedback text, app status log, phone information
  • Providing the Community feature, where guardians share routines and react to each other, and reviewing reports — guardian nickname, Community items
  • Serving ads, measuring ad performance and preventing fraud — Google uses the advertising items

Who can see what you share in Community

Routines, posts and comments shared in Community are visible to other signed-in guardians, and other guardians can take them as their own routines. Your guardian nickname is shown as the author; your email address and sign-in information are not shown. The name used to call the Elumi is replaced with 'Elumi' when posted. Please make sure faces, name tags and addresses are not visible in photos.

3. Retention and Use Period of Personal Information

  • When a member withdraws, the email address, date and time of most recent sign-in and number of sign-ins, tokens used to keep the member signed in, and the routines created by that guardian are destroyed without delay.
  • Elumi information and Elumi phone link information remain if another guardian is still linked, and are destroyed once no linked guardian remains. Stars collected by the Elumi and routines created by other guardians remain as they are.
  • Feedback text, app status logs and phone information are destroyed without delay when the member withdraws. They may be deleted earlier, once the problem has been reviewed.
  • Routines, posts and comments shared in Community, and records of reactions, reports and hiding, are destroyed without delay when the guardian takes them down or withdraws. Routines that other guardians have already taken remain as those guardians' routines.
  • Where a law sets a separate retention period, the information is kept for that period.

4. Provision of Personal Information to Third Parties

ELUM does not provide users' personal information to third parties, except in the following cases.

  • When the user has given prior consent
  • When an investigative agency requests it through lawful procedures under the law

Ads in Guardian mode are collected directly by Google from the app (see 5. Advertising service below). ELUM does not hand users' information over to Google for advertising.

Advertising service (Google AdMob)

In Guardian mode, ads are displayed through Google LLC's advertising service (Google AdMob).

  • Ads appear in Guardian mode (Home, the drafts list, the credits notice) as banner, native and rewarded ads (ads that give credits when watched to the end), and do not appear in Elumi mode or while creating a routine.
  • Information entered in the app, such as the Elumi's name, routines and card content, is not used for ads or passed to Google.
  • On iOS, you can decline in the tracking permission prompt shown at first launch; if you decline, non-personalized ads are shown. You can change this in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. If you decline tracking on iOS, the advertising identifier is not used and only non-personalized ads are shown.

  • How Google uses advertising information is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Please do not include personal information in situation descriptions

Situation descriptions and answers to follow-up questions written by the guardian are sent to the companies above as they are, without modification. Please do not write information that can identify a person, such as names, phone numbers or addresses. The original text is also kept on the ELUM server so that the guardian can check and edit it later.

7. Personal Information of Persons Under 14 (만 14세 미만 아동)

The party who creates and uses an account in ELUM is the guardian (legal representative). Multiple guardians (including family members and social workers) can be linked to one Elumi, and all linked guardians enter the Elumi's information and view routines together. The Elumi does not create an account and does not sign in directly. To enter information on the Elumi's behalf, each guardian must fall under one of the grounds shown in the app (legal representative, legal guardian (후견인), or consent of the Elumi themselves).

The Elumi's name (nickname), character and support goals are entered directly by the guardian. In other words, we do not collect personal information from the person under 14 themselves, and we process it only within the scope entered by the legal representative. Any linked guardian can edit this information in the app, and the most recently changed value is kept. A routine can be edited or deleted only by the guardian who created it.

8. Rights of Data Subjects and How to Exercise Them

  • Users and legal representatives can view, edit or delete their own and the Elumi's information at any time.
  • You can edit it directly in the app's Settings, or request deletion by withdrawing your membership. A guardian can unlink (leave) an Elumi in the app.
  • Requests by email (chan4760@naver.com) are also handled, and you can also request immediate deletion of information kept after withdrawal at this address.

9. Destruction of Personal Information

When the retention period has passed or the purpose of processing has been achieved, the information is destroyed without delay. Electronic files are deleted in a way that makes recovery impossible, and any printed materials are shredded or incinerated.

10. Measures to Ensure the Security of Personal Information

  • Tokens used to keep you signed in are not stored in their original form; only a transformed value is kept, and it is replaced with a new value each time it is renewed.
  • If a token that has already been used is presented again, we treat it as stolen and end all signed-in sessions for that account.
  • All communication between the server and the app is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • The number of people who can access personal information is kept to a minimum.
